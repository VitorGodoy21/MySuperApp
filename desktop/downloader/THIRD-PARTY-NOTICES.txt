My Super App Downloader 0.2.0

The application starts independent external programs. Original binaries and DLLs
are not modified. FFmpeg shared DLLs remain replaceable in _internal\vendor.
Reverse engineering of these components for debugging modifications is not
restricted by this application.

yt-dlp 2026.10.26: Unlicense, with bundled third-party components under their own
licenses. See yt-dlp-LICENSE.txt and yt-dlp-THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt.
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/tree/2026.10.26

Deno 2.9.8: MIT and third-party licenses.
https://github.com/denoland/deno/tree/v2.9.8
See deno-LICENSE.txt and Deno's source for bundled components.

FFmpeg n9.0.2-45-g1e65c6cd5d: BtbN LGPL shared Windows build, not the GPL variant.
See the included FFmpeg license files, ffmpeg -L and ffmpeg -buildconf.
Build recipe: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
Source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/1e65c6cd5d
The full build configuration and dependency sources accompany every public
binary distribution as required by the relevant licenses (see WRITTEN OFFER).

Python 3.12: PSF license, with Tcl/Tk licenses bundled by PyInstaller.
PyInstaller bootloader: GPL with an exception allowing application bundling.
https://www.pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html

WRITTEN OFFER (CORRESPONDING SOURCE):
The complete corresponding source of every LGPL/GPL-licensed component shipped
with this installer (FFmpeg at the exact commit above, every library linked into
its DLLs at the revisions pinned by the BtbN recipe, the recipe itself, LAME,
yt-dlp and Deno), together with SOURCES-MANIFEST.txt listing origin, revision
and SHA256 of each archive, is published as
MySuperAppDownloader-0.2.0-sources.zip next to this installer in the Downloads

the cost of media/transfer. This offer is valid for at least three years after
the last distribution of this version.

UNSIGNED BINARY:
This installer is not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen shows an "unknown
publisher" warning; compare the SHA256 shown in the portal with the downloaded
file before choosing "More info" > "Run anyway".
